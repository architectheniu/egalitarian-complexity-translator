## The Rupture: Truth Detached from Biological Working Memory

On November 20, 2026, mathematics crossed an irreversible threshold: a 17.4-million-line formalization in Lean 4 claiming a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and Dirichlet GRH compiled cleanly and passed independent kernel replay — yet no individual human mind can hold the entire construction in working memory at once.

For 2,500 years, *truth* and *human comprehension* were treated as coextensive. Now, formal verification can succeed in silicon before human intuition exists in biology. If we answer this hyperdensity only with opaque chat summaries that smooth over fractures and invent glue where they lose track, humanity splits into a narrow caste of machine operators and everyone else wandering inside incomprehensible magic.

## Two Symmetries of Egalitarianism

**Egalitarian Complexity Translator (ECT)** is an open protocol (**OSZP — Open Semantic Zoom Protocol**) and reference cognitive microscope anchored in two symmetries:

1. **Egalitarian on the Side of Silicon (Cross-Model Neutrality)**  
   Every capsule strictly isolates its **Formal Core IR** (`formal-core-ir.json`) from human prose translations. Any AI architecture — cloud or local, open-weights or proprietary — reads the exact same dependency skeleton, pinned formal anchors, and epistemic verification record without corporate lock-in.

2. **Egalitarian on the Side of Biology (Continuous Semantic Zoom + Mandatory Honesty)**  
   Every node in that graph renders across five calibrated resolution lenses (`L0` Story → `L1` Intuitive Architect → `L2` Systems Engineer → `L3` Domain Researcher → `L4` Formal Kernel). Moving the zoom slider never alters the underlying causal graph, and every simplified lens displays a mandatory **Compression Loss Meter** documenting *what was omitted* and *where the metaphor begins to break*.

## Four Architectural Layers

1. **Formal Core (`Graph / IR`)**: Typed DAG of strata, components, formal interfaces, global cuts, and pinned source proofs (`L4` anchor).
2. **Semantic Zoom Engine**: Topology-preserving multi-resolution lenses (`L0`–`L4`) in multiple languages (`en`, `pl`, and extensible to others).
3. **Compression Loss Meter**: Quantitative fidelity score, compression ratio, topology status, omitted mechanisms, and mandatory fracture boundary (`where_metaphor_breaks`).
4. **Human Interface (Audiovisual Microscope)**: Interactive cognitive instrument in the browser combining spatial graph flow, Web Audio resonance, guided audiovisual walkthroughs, real mathematical micro-simulations, and live `.capsule.jsonld` portability.
